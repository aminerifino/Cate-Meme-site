Decode the Language of Solana

CA (Contract Address)Basics

The unique cryptographic identifier on Solana for a token mint. $CALI's official CA is 8k4sBtEeK4pf26noKqApv8NBTnuSJcbdwpKYknk5PbAA.

Bonding CurveTrading

An automated mathematical pricing curve used by launchpads like Pump.fun where price increases predictably as tokens are purchased until market graduation.

HODLSlang

"Hold On for Dear Life" — refusing to sell your tokens during market volatility because of long-term conviction.

Diamond Paws 🐾Slang

The feline evolution of Diamond Hands. Unshakable holders who hold through every dip to reach the ninth life.

Market Cap (MCap)Trading

Total supply of tokens multiplied by current token price. Represents the total valuation of the ecosystem.

Seed PhraseBasics

A sequence of 12 or 24 words that acts as the master key to your wallet. If anyone gets it, they own your funds. Never share it.

WAGMISlang

"We're All Gonna Make It" — community rally cry celebrating solidarity, optimism, and shared victory in the meme economy.

DEX (Decentralized Exchange)Trading

A peer-to-peer marketplace (like Raydium or Pump.fun) where swaps occur on-chain without an intermediary bank or broker.

RaidSlang

Coordinated community action on social media (especially X) to like, retweet, and reply to raise token awareness.

Non-CustodialBasics

Wallets like Phantom where you solely control private keys, rather than trusting a centralized exchange.

SlippageTrading

The difference between the expected price of a trade and the executed price due to rapid on-chain price movements.

The Ninth LifeSlang

The legendary community-driven renaissance of Cate on Solana. Eight lives were hers; the ninth is yours.
